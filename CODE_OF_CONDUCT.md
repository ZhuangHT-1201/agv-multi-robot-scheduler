# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people,
and create an environment where contributors of different backgrounds can work
productively.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or sustained
disruption of project discussions.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issue trackers, pull requests,
review comments, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports should be handled privately by the
maintainers.
